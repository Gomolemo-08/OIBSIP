Her story

Curiosity against the odds

Motlalepula Agnes Mogwasi was born in Mareetsane, North West in 1982, some people leave behind achievements, while others leave behind a legacy. Our mother blessed us with both. She was a remarkable woman whose intelligence, compassion, and strength touched every life she encountered. Whether she was solving complex problems as an ICT specialist, leading with excellence in the retail industry, expressing creativity as a beautician, or simply being a loving mother.

She gave every role her whole heart. She believed in working hard, learning continuously, and treating every person with kindness and respect. Her career reflected her determination and versatility. She embraced technology with confidence, served customers with excellence, and helped others see their own beauty, not only on the outside but by encouraging confidence from within. She showed us that success is not measured only by titles, but by the positive difference we make in the lives of others.

Above every accomplishment, she was our mother. She lovingly raised my sister and I with unwavering devotion, endless patience, and unconditional love. She celebrated our victories, comforted us through our struggles, and taught us the values that will remain with us forever: integrity, resilience, humility, faith, and compassion. Through her example, she showed us what it means to love selflessly and to persevere through life's challenges with grace.

She had a beautiful spirit that made people feel welcomed and valued. Her smile brought comfort, her wisdom offered guidance, and her presence made every room warmer. The love she poured into her family, friends, and community continues to live on in every heart she touched. Although her earthly journey has come to an end, her story does not end here. It lives on in the lessons she taught, the lives she uplifted, the memories we cherish, and the two daughters who proudly carry forward her strength, kindness, and legacy. We are who we are because of her love.

Thank you, Mom, for every sacrifice, every prayer, every embrace, and every lesson. Your life was a beautiful testimony of purpose, resilience, and unconditional love. We will miss you beyond words, love you beyond measure, and carry you in our hearts for the rest of our lives.

Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood.

Motlalepula Mogwasi, attributed
